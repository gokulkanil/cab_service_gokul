Good to know before you go

01

The Bandipur night ban

NH 766 through Bandipur, towards Mysuru and Bengaluru, is closed to most traffic from 9 PM to 6 AM. We'll time your departure around it.

02

Monsoon months

June to September brings heavy rain and roaring waterfalls. Some spots and treks may close. Ask us what's open before you plan.

03

Edakkal Caves

Usually closed on Mondays, and the last stretch is a steep climb. Go early to beat the queue.

04

Chembra trek

Entry is managed by the Forest Department and is sometimes closed. Check with us a day before.

05

Best time to visit

October to May is pleasant and mostly dry. December and January mornings are cool and misty.

How do I book a cab?

Call us or send a WhatsApp message with your pickup point, date, time and number of passengers. We'll confirm the fare and share your driver's details.

Do you offer airport and railway pickups?

Yes. We cover Calicut and Kannur airports, Kozhikode railway station, and Mysuru and Bengaluru. Share your flight or train details and your driver will be waiting when you arrive.

How is the fare decided?

It depends on the distance, the type of trip and any waiting time. We tell you the full fare before the trip, so there are no surprises at the end.

How many people fit in an Etios?

Up to 4 passengers plus the driver, with a large boot for luggage. If your group is bigger, call us and we'll help you plan.

Can you plan a sightseeing day for us?

Yes. Tell us what you enjoy (waterfalls, treks, temples, wildlife) and how much time you have. We'll suggest a route that avoids backtracking.

Do you drive early in the morning or late at night?

Yes, early and late pickups can be arranged. For Mysuru and Bengaluru trips we plan around the Bandipur night travel ban.
